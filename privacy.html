Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This website is the personal portfolio of Tina Yaghchian. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit it and how it is used.

Information collected

  • Analytics. This site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, your approximate location, and your device and browser type. I use it to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Google handles this data under its Privacy Policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, including your IP address, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it requests them.
  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark themes, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to me.
  • Messages you send. If you email me, I receive your message and email address and use them only to reply.

What this site does not do

This site has no user accounts, contact forms, or advertising. I do not sell your personal information.

How long information is kept

Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting of this site's Google Analytics account. Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use the opt-out add-on above. To ask about, correct, or delete information you have sent me, email tyaghchi@uwaterloo.ca.

Other websites

This site links to other websites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and Instagram. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tyaghchi@uwaterloo.ca.
